Policy

Where We Stand

Five issues. One direction.

01 Surveillance

“8,300% Increase in Flock Cameras”

The ASP has promised a drastic increase in all surveillance equipment, including but not limited to flock cameras, drones, and hidden cameras covering as much area as possible. The ASP threatens to increase the number from the current 120,000 to nearly 10 million cameras throughout the United States.

02 Free Expression

“Bill of Rights Burned”

According to the Grand Admiral Harrington, the freedom of expression in this country is far too great. It has led to, quote, “death, destruction, and suffering among the good people of the United States.” Harrington claims that if there is a decrease in the freedom of speech, assembly, and press, the people will be less “riled up” and more willing to “comply.”

03 Immigration

“Total Border Shutdown”

The Security Party states that the drastic increase in immigration in the past decade has contributed to the downfall of the country. They said that with a total border shutdown, from every country, no matter if a neighboring country or across the ocean, the so-called issue with the country would be solved overnight.

04 Economy

“Nationalize the Economy”

The American Security Party advocates for a highly planned economy focused on class collaboration. The oil and gas industry will become nationalized. Labor unions will be made illegal and corporations will be forced to collaborate by the national government. Wages, for the most part, will be controlled by the government.

05 Foreign Policy

“America Only”

Foreign policy will follow a strict policy of America only. It is in America's best interest to not care at all about the opinions or needs of the rest of the world. If a country gets in our way, we will use our extremely strong military to exert our authority. If Canada gets too weak, we will invade them. They have lots of oil and gas and they are easy victims.
